04 · AI use cases

Three AI use cases turn carbon data into decisions

Each feeds the next: clean data trains prediction, and prediction drives optimization.

01 · GenAI / NLP

Carbon data intelligence

Classify BOM and supplier records; suggest emission-factor matches.

Output: clean, matched carbon data

02 · Supervised ML

Carbon prediction

Estimate embodied carbon for design and supplier options before build.

Output: kg CO₂e per option, with confidence

03 · ML + optimization

Decision optimization

Rank options on cost, performance, availability and carbon.

Output: recommended trade-offs

Next option · agentic monitoring

Flag supplier or design changes; people approve every sourcing and design decision.

Use cases and technologies per Davenport’s AI strategy framework (Babson College, 2025).

06 · Build vs. buy

Build what differentiates NVIDIA; buy standard carbon data

Proprietary product and supplier context is the advantage; emission-factor data is not.

Build / customize · owned by NVIDIA
  • Predictive carbon models
  • Multi-objective optimization
  • Integration with product workflows
  • Supplier and component intelligence
  • Internal decision interface

Why: uses proprietary design and supplier data competitors cannot copy.

Buy / partner · vendor systems
  • Lifecycle inventory databases
  • External emission-factor datasets
  • Industry benchmark datasets
  • Verification and assurance support

Why: standard carbon data is costly to recreate and not a differentiator.

Build-vs-buy criteria per Davenport’s AI strategy framework (Babson College, 2025).

08 · Responsible AI

No supplier or design change happens solely because the model predicts lower carbon.
